Sinigang

Sinigang is a Popular Filipino Soup Dish Featuring a Tamarid Rich Dish where the meat is boiled alongside the tamarids.

Ingredients

  • 1/2kg Pork Kasim
  • 1 Pack Sinigang Mix or 1/2 kilo tamarind paste
  • 4pcs Gabi
  • 5pcs Okra
  • 5 Stem Kangkong
  • 2pcs. Tomatoes

Procedures

  1. Wash the Pork and Boil in Medium High Heat for 45 mins
  2. Add the Sliced Tomatoes and The Tamarid Paste or Sinigang Mix and wait for it to simmer to 10mins
  3. Add Gabi and Okra
  4. Before Finishing add all the leafy vegetables to prevent overcooking
  5. Enjoy your Siningang served with Rice
