Tofu Sisig

Tofu Sisig or Tokwa Sisig is a Kapampangan sisig dish that contains deep fried tofu with mayo and spice dressing.

Ingredients

  • Firm or Extra Firm Tofu
  • 2 pcs bell pepper
  • 1 pcs. long pepper
  • 1 pcs. onion
  • Butter
  • Cooking Oil
  • 1 Cup Calamansi Juice
  • 1 Cup liverspread
  • 1 Cup Soy Sauce
  • Mayonnaise
  • Ground Black pepper

Procedures

  1. In a bowl combine Mayonnaise, soy sauce, calamansi juice and black pepper.
  2. Add Liver spread, and Stir until well blended. Set aside.
  3. Heat cooking oil in a pan over mediun heat. Deep fry tofu until golden brown.
  4. Remove the tofu from the pan and place on a plate with paper towels to remove excess oil.
  5. Leave atleast 1 tbsp. of oil in the pan and add butter. After the butter melts, add the chopped onion, bell peppers, and green chilli peppers. Cook until the veggies softens.
  6. Transfer all the Ingredients in a mixing bowl and mix well. Add extra raw onions for texture.
  7. Heat a sizzling plate, add butter, and add the sisig mixture when the butter melts. We can also serve the sisig on a plate.
